# Getting started

These sections are designed to be your compass in the vast landscape of our automation tool. Whether you are a first-time user or a seasoned builder, this guide will help you navigate our ecosystem, move from initial setup to advanced deployment, and master the art of efficient automation.

* * *

### 🎓 Learning Path & Resources

Explore the subpages below to begin your journey. We recommend starting with the **Quickstart** if you’re ready to build immediately, or the **Basics** if you prefer a solid theoretical foundation.

### 💡 Tips for Success

> **Start Small:** Use the **Quickstart** to solve a simple problem first. Once you see the "magic" happen, the **Basics** and **Training** sections will make much more sense.
> 
> **Stay Organized:** Check out the **No-Code Best Practices** early on. It’s much easier to build a clean workflow from the start than to fix a messy one later!
